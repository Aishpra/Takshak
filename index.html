Good to know

Questions,
answered.

How do I buy a ticket?+

Choose the current ticket phase above and tap “Book now”. You will be taken to Khalti to complete the payment.

Does a Khalti click confirm my ticket?+

No. The website records interest when someone clicks the Khalti button. Completed sales are confirmed through the Khalti or organizer sales report.

Can I enter the giveaway without buying a ticket?+

Yes. Submit the giveaway form and agree to the rules. You can also choose whether you want future event updates.

Will ticket prices change?+

Yes. Tickets are released in phases, so the price increases when the current phase sells out or closes.

Where is the event?+

Dharan Cricket Stadium, Dharan. Venue and entry information will be updated here and on Takshak’s social pages.
