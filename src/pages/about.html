About Takshak

The organisation
behind the night.

Takshak Entertainment creates, organises, promotes and powers live experiences in the East of Nepal — Takshak Live is the first proof of what that looks like.

Who we are

An event organiser,
not a portfolio.

Takshak is a Dharan-based event organiser. Artists are the reason people show up, but Takshak is the reason the night happens at all — booking the lineup, producing the stage, and putting every detail in place so the only thing anyone has to do is show up and feel it.

We're early. Takshak Live, our flagship production, is the first large-scale night we've put our name on. Everything on this site — the roster, the systems, the way we talk about a show — is built to scale past that first night, not just describe it.

What we do

Four things,
done properly.

  • 01Artist bookingWe work directly with artists and their teams to bring the right act to the right stage.
  • 02Event productionSound, lighting, staging and the hundred small logistics that make a show feel effortless.
  • 03PromotionGetting the right crowd in front of the right event, from the first announcement to the last ticket.
  • 04Experience designEverything from the ticket to the merch table is designed on purpose, not left to default.

Our approach

Built for the crowd
that shows up.

Dharan, Itahari, Biratnagar and everywhere in between — that's who we're building for. Not every event has to happen in Kathmandu for it to matter, and not every act worth seeing needs a capital-city stage to sell out.

We'd rather do one night properly than three nights half-built. That's the whole approach: fewer, better, done right.

Our vision

Every region deserves
a real stage.

Nepal's live scene doesn't have to live in one city. Takshak exists to bring proper productions — real sound, real lighting, real lineups — to the East, and to keep raising the bar on what a "local" event can feel like.

Takshak Live is night one. If you're an artist, a venue, or a brand who wants to build something with us, we'd like to hear from you.
